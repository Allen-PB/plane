# Office scene day and night

Dev release `office-scene-20261022-01` replaces the earlier whole-page dark mode. The current day artwork is retained byte-for-byte (SHA256 b82f0fc48d9e224b206e6f305f2f9304d1d76c80ea89c4a935e34307698e6780). A matching night artwork shows dark surroundings beyond the glass and warm interior illumination. Page chrome, directory, header, navigation, and management panels keep their existing appearance.

Automatic mode follows Asia/Manila: day from 06:00 inclusive until 18:00 exclusive; night otherwise. The client uses server time to account for an incorrect device clock, schedules the next boundary without reloading, and refreshes on focus/visibility. Admin and Super Admin accounts can choose Automatic, Day, or Night. The setting is persisted on the office, audited, and returned to every office member. Employee writes are rejected by the backend, and employees do not see the control. Existing office polling propagates shared overrides to other clients within the normal polling interval.

Each assigned desk receives a non-interactive light glow while its active employee has an open attendance session. The glow follows `timed_in_at`, independently of browser online status, and disappears after manual or automatic 48-hour timeout. The current renderer's calibrated desk targets, panning behavior, department labels, avatars, and camera transforms are preserved.
